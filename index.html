QUESTIONS

Everything you're wondering.

Do I need any technical knowledge to work with you?

None at all. You tell us how your business works in plain English; we handle the design, build, deployment, and ongoing management. Most of our clients have never written a line of code.

How long does it take to get a system live?

Most systems launch within 2–4 weeks of your discovery call. Simple voice or chat agents can go live in days; larger custom workflow systems can take longer depending on complexity.

Will the AI sound robotic to my customers?

No. Every agent is trained on your business — your services, your tone, your pricing, your FAQs. Customers get fast, natural, accurate answers, and anything sensitive is routed to a human.

What happens when the AI doesn't know the answer?

It gracefully hands off. Agents are configured with clear escalation rules — when a question is outside their scope, they collect the details and route it to the right person on your team immediately.

Is my business data safe?

Yes. Your data stays yours. We follow security best practices, use encrypted connections throughout, and never use your business data to train anything outside your own system.

How much does it cost?

Every system is custom, so pricing depends on scope. After your free discovery call and automation audit, you'll get a clear fixed proposal — what it costs, what it saves, and the expected payback period. No surprises.
